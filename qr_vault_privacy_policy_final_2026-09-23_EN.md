# QR Vault Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: September 23, 2026**

Intelligence Utility (the "Company") values the privacy of users of **QR Vault** (the "App"). The Company provides this Privacy Policy to explain how information may be processed when you use the App and to comply with applicable laws and Google Play's privacy and data disclosure requirements.

This Privacy Policy applies to the QR Vault mobile application provided by the Company.

## 1. Purpose of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed while using QR Vault, the purposes for which such information may be used, how third-party services may be involved, and information regarding data security, retention, and deletion.

## 2. Information Collected and Processed

QR Vault processes information only to the extent necessary to provide its features.

### 2.1 QR-Related Information

When you scan a QR code or enter QR-related content into the App, that content may be processed to provide the App's functionality.

Whether QR-related information is stored, and how long it is retained, may vary depending on the App's actual features and the user's choices.

The Company does not sell QR-related information for advertising purposes or provide it to third parties. However, third-party services described below may separately process certain information to provide advertising and payment services.

### 2.2 Advertising-Related Information

QR Vault uses **Google AdMob** to provide advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or share certain information for purposes such as providing advertisements, measuring advertising performance, analytics, and fraud prevention. Such information may include:

- Device IP address
- Information about user interactions with the App, such as app launches, taps, advertisements, or video views
- Diagnostic information related to the App and SDK performance
- Device and account identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID
- Advertising impression and advertising-related information

The exact scope of information collected or processed may vary depending on the version of the Google Mobile Ads SDK used and the App's configuration.

The use of advertising identifiers and the availability of personalized advertising may be managed through Android device settings and Google's applicable policies.

Data processed through Google Mobile Ads SDK is subject to Google's Privacy Policy and applicable advertising policies.

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Google AdMob Privacy and Data Disclosure: https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure

### 2.3 In-App Purchase Information

QR Vault provides a **one-time in-app purchase through Google Play Billing to unlock premium features**.

This purchase is provided as a **one-time, non-consumable premium product**, not as a subscription.

The Company does not directly collect or store detailed payment-method information such as credit card numbers or bank account information. Payment processing is handled through Google Play's billing system.

The App may process purchase-related information provided through Google Play Billing in order to verify the purchase and provide premium access. This may include information such as the product identifier, purchase status, purchase time, and purchase token, as necessary to process the transaction.

The processing of payment-related data is subject to Google's Privacy Policy and Google Play's payment-related policies.

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Google Play Billing Documentation: https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing

## 3. Purposes of Information Use

The Company uses processed information only for the following purposes:

1. Providing and maintaining QR Vault's core features
2. Verifying premium purchases and providing access to premium features
3. Providing advertisements and measuring advertising performance
4. Identifying and resolving service stability, performance, and error-related issues
5. Preventing misuse and advertising-related fraud
6. Responding to user inquiries and support requests
7. Complying with applicable laws and Google Play policies

The Company does not sell or arbitrarily provide information collected through QR Vault outside the scope permitted by the user's consent or applicable laws.
